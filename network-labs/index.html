Address & test

Give each device an IPv4 address. Open its PING window and watch a glowing signal travel to another device and back. Explore automatic address allocation.

LAB 03
SPLIT · ROUTE · REASSEMBLE

Switch packets

Choose a virtual photo, document, spreadsheet or video. Watch it split into numbered packets, take different routes, reform at the receiver and save to its hard disk.

Your challenge: explain why packets arrive out of order.

Find a website

Type a web address and press Go. Watch the browser ask DNS for an IP address, request the page from the web server, and display the returned HTML.
